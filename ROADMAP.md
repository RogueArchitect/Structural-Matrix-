Structural Matrix — Project Roadmap
This roadmap outlines the planned development of the Structural Matrix framework.
It covers documentation, tooling, research expansion, and future applications of the method.

The goal is to evolve the Structural Matrix from a conceptual framework into a fully operational analytical toolkit.
