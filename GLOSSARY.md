
Structural Matrix — Glossary of Terms
A unified vocabulary for the Structural Matrix and the Consilient Structure Filter.

This glossary defines the core terminology used throughout the Structural Matrix framework.
All terms refer to behavioural structure, not linguistic meaning.

A
Anchor
A low‑entropy, high‑stability symbol that consistently appears at the start of cycles or structural units.
Functions as a reset point or stabilising reference.

Architecture
The total behavioural structure of a system, defined by roles, transitions, entropy, motifs, and (optionally) 3D cohesion.

B
Boundary Frame
A symbol or symbol‑pair that encloses or segments content. Often appears at module edges.

Burst / Cluster Burst
A dense, high‑entropy region of repeated or rapidly varying symbols.

C
Cluster
A group of repeated or closely related symbols forming a dense content region.

Content Block
A high‑entropy region representing the “payload” of the system.

Constructed System
A system built from modular templates with low entropy and repeated structural patterns.

Cycle
A repeating sequence of roles or transitions, often anchored by A → T → C → X → A.

Cohesion (3D)
A measure of spatial proximity between symbols in 3D systems (e.g., DNA loops, domains).

D
Drift / Natural Drift
Irregular movement of roles, especially anchors, characteristic of natural systems.

Dependency
A structural requirement governing how a role behaves (e.g., content depends on transitions).

E
Engineered System
A system with predictable transitions, stable anchors, and repeating cycles.

Entropy
A measure of unpredictability in symbol behaviour.
Used to detect anchors, blocks, terminators, and cascades.

Entropy Cascade
A pattern where structure dissolves into noise over time.

F
Frame
A structural boundary that encloses or segments content.

Floating Anchor
An anchor that appears inconsistently or shifts position across cycles.

H
Hybrid Motif
A mixed behavioural pattern combining natural and engineered features.

M
Matrix (Structural Matrix)
The full analytical framework combining SV, BV, SCV, roles, motifs, and classification.

Modular Block
A repeated structural template characteristic of constructed systems.

Motif
A recurring behavioural signature detected by the Structural Matrix.

N
Natural System
A system with organic variation, irregular transitions, and high entropy.

Null Motif
A pattern with no structure, no roles, and no transitions.

R
Random System
A system with no detectable structure or behavioural consistency.

Role
A functional behaviour category assigned to a symbol (Anchor, Frame, Transition, Content, Terminator, 3D‑Dominant).

S
Segmentation
Division of a system into structural blocks or modules.

Signal
The numeric representation of a symbolic system used for analysis.

Spatial Cohesion Vector (SCV)
A vector describing 3D proximity relationships between symbols.

Structural Behaviour
The measurable patterns of transitions, entropy, roles, and motifs.

System Classification
The process of determining whether a system is natural, engineered, constructed, random, or 3D‑driven.

T
Terminator
A symbol that consistently ends sequences or cycles.
Characterised by entropy collapse.

Transition
A symbol that bridges one structural state to another.
Medium entropy, directional behaviour.

Template
A repeated structural pattern used in constructed systems.

V
Vibration
The conceptual model treating text as a frequency pattern rather than linguistic content.

Bonus Term
Consilient Structure Filter
The philosophical foundation of the Structural Matrix:
a system that extracts universal structure across domains by filtering out semantics and isolating pure behavioural dynamics.
