Structural Matrix — Glossary of Terms
This glossary defines the core terminology used throughout the Structural Matrix framework.
All terms refer to behavioural roles, not linguistic meaning.

A
Anchor (A)
A symbol that consistently appears at the start of cycles.
Low entropy, high positional stability.
Acts as the system’s reset point.

Architecture
The overall structural behaviour of a system, defined by roles, transitions, entropy, and motifs.

B
Boundary Frame (F)
A symbol or pair of symbols that enclose or segment content.
Often appears at the edges of modules.

Burst (Cluster Burst)
A dense repetition of content symbols forming a high‑entropy block.

C
Cluster
A group of repeated symbols forming a dense content block.

Content Block (C)
A region of high‑frequency or repeated symbols.
Represents the “payload” of the system.

Constructed System
A system built from modular templates with low entropy and repeated patterns.

Cycle
A repeating sequence of transitions, typically A → T → C → X → A.

D
Drift (Natural Drift)
Irregular movement of roles, especially anchors, in natural systems.

Dependency
A structural requirement that defines how a role behaves (e.g., content depends on transitions).

E
Engineered System
A system with predictable transitions, stable anchors, and repeating cycles.

Entropy
A measure of unpredictability or variation in symbol behaviour.

Entropy Cascade
A pattern where structure dissolves into noise over time.

F
Frame (F)
A structural boundary that encloses or segments content.

Floating Anchor
An anchor that appears inconsistently or in varying positions.

H
Hoax / Random System
A system with no structure, no roles, and no consistent transitions.

Hybrid Motif
A mixed behavioural pattern combining natural and engineered features.

M
Matrix (Structural Matrix)
The full set of roles, transitions, and dependencies that define a system’s behaviour.

Modular Block
A repeated structural template found in constructed systems.

Motif
A recurring behavioural pattern detected by the Structural Matrix.

N
Natural System
A system with organic variation, irregular transitions, and high entropy.

Null Motif
A pattern with no structure, no roles, and no transitions.

R
Random System
A system with no detectable structure or behavioural consistency.

Role
A functional behaviour category assigned to a symbol (Anchor, Frame, Transition, Content, Terminator).

S
Segmentation
Division of a system into structural blocks or modules.

Signal
The numeric representation of a symbolic system used for analysis.

Structural Behaviour
The measurable patterns of transitions, clusters, and roles.

System Classification
The process of determining whether a system is natural, engineered, constructed, or random.

T
Terminator (X)
A symbol that consistently ends sequences or cycles.

Transition (T)
A symbol that bridges one structural state to another.
Medium entropy, directional behaviour.

Template
A repeated structural pattern used in constructed systems.

V
Vibration
The conceptual model treating text as a frequency pattern rather than linguistic content.
